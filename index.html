Beginner full-body circuit

2 rounds · Rest 45–75 seconds between exercises. Move slowly and keep good form.

10 chair or bodyweight squats · 8 wall/incline push-ups · 10 glute bridges · 8 reverse lunges per side · 20-second plank · 8 bird dogs per side
